For LSR, start with access and a baseline, then limit months 2 – 3 to index and metadata cleanup. Reuse the tested UNICARB template fixes from month 4 and follow with product and decarbonisation content.

QUALITATIVE OUTLOOK

When to look for progress

Planning expectations

These are planning windows. Timing depends on access, publishing and recrawl. The plan assumes a further 1 – 3 months for inquiries that become orders.

Measurement protocol and year-one targets

Use 40 – 60 fixed prompts per language in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot. Repeat runs and report four-week averages. Review available AI referral data in GA4 and citation data in Bing Webmaster Tools.

Year-one aims are to sustain 80+ readiness, index eligible pages in the correct language, remove unwanted sitemap URLs and publish approved figures and sheets across both portfolios. Compare clicks and inquiries with the baseline, and look for a rising share of AI answers mentioning UNICARB. Set exact growth percentages after baseline measurement.

The audit’s indicative ranges are 2 – 8 weeks for initial rankings on new or reworked pages and 4 – 12 weeks after publication for external mentions or AI citations. Check for steadier inquiry growth in months 6 – 9 against the baseline.

EXECUTION & NEXT STEPS

What we need to start.

UNICARB leads the programme. Approve the focus, provide access and give the delivery team a clear path to publish technical facts.

KICKOFF DECISIONS

  1. Confirm the commercial focus

    Agree on the first markets, product groups and qualified inquiry criteria. Share known competitors and confirm the Polish contact and Gdańsk office.

  2. Grant access in week 1

    Provide Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, GA4, WordPress admin and hosting or Cloudflare access. Confirm who can change the shared theme.

  3. Assign approvals and resources

    Book the German technical editor. Name the engineer who approves publishable typical values, TDS and SDS, drinking-water claims and case studies. Agree on copy and trade media budget.

PHASE TWO · LSR MATERIALS

Baseline in month 1, then clean the index and localize metadata. From month 4, reuse shared heading, footer, alt text and form fixes. Add product figures and biocarbon content for steel and foundry. Route activated-carbon applications to UNICARB.

REPORTING CADENCE

Weekly change log, errors and decisions. Monthly baseline comparison and next work by language. Quarterly crawl, competitor review and prompt-set review.

Open approvals and delivery risks

Confirm EN 12915, DVGW and PZH applicability and evidence, reactivation or take-back availability, and permission to publish comparisons and PFAS or biogas cases. Use primary sources and engineering approval for regulatory and product claims. Native speakers approve localized copy.

Agree on agency turnaround and access to the language and SEO plugins. Missing product data or delayed theme work can shift publishing. Review current traffic before changing URLs. The audit allows 3 – 8 weeks for redirects to settle and notes potential volatility from search updates.
